# Contributing

This repository is publicly available research software, released as a snapshot of an
internal research codebase. It is maintained on a best-effort basis.

- Bug reports and reproducibility issues are welcome via GitHub issues.
- Pull requests may be accepted at our discretion; large changes are unlikely to be merged.
- By submitting a contribution you (a) confirm you have the right to submit it, and
  (b) agree it is provided under the Ammonix Research License (see `LICENSE.md`).
- Commercial licensing questions: licensing@ammonix.ai.
